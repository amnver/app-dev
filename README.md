# My Favorite Movies/Series

## A Walk To Remember
![alt text](awtr.jpg)

A romantic drama based on Nicholas Sparks’ novel of the same name. Set in a small North Carolina town, it follows Landon Carter, a popular but rebellious high school student, who is forced to participate in community service after a prank goes wrong. There, he meets Jamie Sullivan, the quiet, kind daughter of the town’s minister. Despite their differences, Landon and Jamie develop an unexpected romance that transforms Landon’s outlook on life. However, their love faces a heartbreaking test when Jamie reveals she has leukemia.

- - - 

**Directed by** Adam Shankman 

**Main Cast**
Shane West _as_ Landon Carter and Mandy Moore _as_ Jamie Sullivan
